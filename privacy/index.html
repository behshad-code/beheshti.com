Privacy

How this site handles visitor information.

Last updated November 10, 2026

About the site

Beheshti.com is a family-owned editorial website. Visitors do not need an account or payment, and there are no on-site contact forms or mailing-list signups.

Hosting and analytics

GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process IP addresses for security and delivery. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement. We use GoatCounter for aggregate page visits and fixed interaction categories, with Beheshti-specific labels within the shared behshad.com analytics account. GoatCounter does not set analytics cookies or store raw visitor IP addresses. See GoatCounter’s privacy information.

Custom events do not include search terms, visitor notes, or personal information. We do not use advertising pixels or sell visitor information.

External services

Google Fonts delivers website typography and receives the technical information needed for delivery. Research sources and external websites have their own privacy policies. The site publishes selected photos and illustrations; it does not offer access to private family archives.

Choices and contact

Privacy features and content blockers can restrict third-party analytics. No visitor account is required. For privacy questions use the contact options at behshad.com. This notice will be updated when the site’s practices change.
